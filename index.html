AI Agriculture Marketplace

KrishiSetu – AI Powered Agriculture Marketplace

ReactFastAPIPythonPostgreSQLJWT

Overview

KrishiSetu is an end-to-end digital platform engineered to bridge the gap between rural farmers and commercial vendors. It replaces traditional middlemen with a transparent, AI-driven marketplace that provides real-time pricing, crop analytics, and farming guidance.

Key Modules & Architecture

AI Crop & Yield Engine

Predictive modules analyzing soil parameters, weather data, and seasonal trends to recommend high-yield crops and optimal harvesting schedules.

Harvest Discovery & Marketplace

Empowers farmers to create verified crop harvest listings with pictures, pricing, and quantities for direct vendor bidding.

Multilingual Support

Accessibility-first interface supporting local regional languages so non-tech-savvy farmers can navigate effortless crop guidance.

Secure JWT Auth & DB

Role-based JWT authentication separating farmer and vendor portals, with PostgreSQL managing user sessions and transaction logs.

HangHive – Real Time Communication Platform

ReactFastAPIPythonPostgreSQLJWTWebSockets

Overview

HangHive is a high-performance full-stack communication platform supporting instant messaging, group channels, media posting, and real-time audio/video streaming. Built with WebSockets and FastAPI, it achieves sub-100ms messaging latencies across distributed client instances.

Core Capabilities

Real-Time Messaging & WebSockets

Bi-directional WebSocket connection pipeline delivering instant text messaging, typing indicators, read receipts, and online status.

Voice & Video Call Streaming

Group voice and video calling infrastructure enabling multi-party live conferencing with mute, camera toggle, and screen share controls.

Channels & Video Feeds

Creation of public and private channels, media attachments, video post feeds, and persistent chat history backed by PostgreSQL.

Role-Based Access Control

Strict admin/moderator/member permissions powered by JWT authorization for secure channel and server management.
